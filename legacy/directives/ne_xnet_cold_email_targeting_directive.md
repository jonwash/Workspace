# NeXNet — Cold Email Targeting Directive

## Company Inclusion Rules

A company is eligible **only if all conditions below are met**.

### Industry (Allowed Only)

- Law Practice  
- Accounting  
- Architecture  
- Engineering  
- Medical Practice  
- Healthcare Services  

All other industries are excluded.

---

### Employee Count

- Minimum: **10**  
- Maximum: **75**  
- Ideal: **15–40**

If exact count is unavailable, infer from LinkedIn or equivalent signals.

---

### Company Age

- **≥ 3 years in operation**

If founding date is unknown, do not disqualify unless evidence suggests a newer business.

---

### Geography

- **United States only**

---

### Business Presence

- Must use a **business email domain**  
- Must have an active website or LinkedIn company page

Exclude:
- Gmail-only businesses  
- No web presence

---

## Contact Inclusion Rules

Each company may contribute **1–2 contacts maximum**.

### Primary Contact Titles

- Owner  
- Founder  
- Managing Partner  
- Partner  
- Principal  
- CEO

---

### Secondary Contact Titles  
*(Only if no primary contact exists)*

- Director of Operations  
- Operations Manager  
- Practice Manager  
- Office Manager  
- COO

---

### Contact Exclusions

Exclude any contact with these titles:

- IT Manager  
- Director of IT  
- CTO  
- SysAdmin  
- Network Administrator  
- Marketing roles  
- Sales roles

---

## Disqualification Rules (Hard Stops)

Exclude immediately if any condition applies:

- Solo practice  
- Fewer than 10 employees  
- Less than 3 years old (if known)  
- Personal email addresses only  
- Retail, restaurant, salon, gym, or e-commerce business

---

## Output Constraints

- Total leads per run: **50–150**  
- Prioritize relevance over volume  
- Do not include analysis, commentary, or scoring

---

**End of Targeting Directive**
